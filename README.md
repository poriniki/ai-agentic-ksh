# The AI-Powered Professional

*From Chat User to AI Operator: practical AI for Marketing, Business and Finance teams*

A 4-hour workshop with homework and projects, teaching Marketing, Business and Finance teams to use AI assistants (Claude, ChatGPT) professionally: in their daily work, and as a managed team capability.

**Audience:** non-technical professionals who already use AI through the web interface.
**Format:** one 4-hour session → homework (~1 week) → individual project + team project → presentations at the next session.
**Core idea:** the goal is to structure how people *think* about AI work, not to tour features. Three mental tools run through everything:

1. **The AI Work Ladder:** Ask → Brief → Equip → Chain → Delegate
2. **The 4Ds:** Delegation · Description · Discernment · Diligence (from Anthropic's AI Fluency course)
3. **The Delegation Matrix:** AI does it · AI drafts, you review · you do it

## Before you use this

Fill these in (search the files for `[date`, `[location`, `[channel`, `fill in`):

- [ ] Your company's approved AI tools and plan → slides 7, 30, 53; `05-resources/feature-map.md`
- [ ] Your company's data rules → slide 53; `05-resources/ai-team-policy-template.md`
- [ ] Due dates, submission location, team channel → `02-homework/`, `03-…`, `04-…`
- [ ] Decide the assignment load (see below)

**Workload note:** homework (~3–4 h) + individual project (~4–6 h) + team project (~6–8 h) adds up to roughly 13–18 hours per person before the next session. That's a lot alongside a full-time job. If the gap between sessions is under two weeks, assign the homework plus **one** of the two projects: the team project if the goal is team adoption, the individual project if it's personal productivity.
