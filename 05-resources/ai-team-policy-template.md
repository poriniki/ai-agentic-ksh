# Team AI Charter — Template

*Course: The AI-Powered Professional*

A one-to-two page agreement on how a team uses AI. Fill it in with your team, keep it short, and review it every quarter. Used in Module 6 and as the starting point for the team project's Team AI Charter.

This is a template, not legal advice. Align it with your company's official IT, security, privacy and legal policies; where they conflict, the company policy wins.

## 6. Disclosure

- We say AI helped when: [e.g. client deliverables, published research, decision documents]

## 7. Roles

- **AI champion:** maintains this charter, the library and shared Projects; runs the monthly share; first point of contact for questions
- **Everyone:** follows the traffic light and review rules; adds good prompts to the library; reports problems

## 8. Learning rhythm

- Monthly 30-minute share: one win, one failure, one new technique
- New team members: onboarding with this charter + the prompt library
